# Archive

Material from early in the project that the benchmark does not use. Kept for the record.

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `dropped_photos/` | The 5 photos cut from the benchmark. They had too many readable books still unlabelled to score fairly. |
| `dropped_photos_ground_truth.json` | Their draft labels. Incomplete, so not usable as an answer key. |
| `original_photos.zip` | All 11 original photos with their original filenames, as first collected. The 6 benchmark photos are in `data/images/`, renamed `a` to `f`. |
| `pilot_claude_sonnet_5_5_low.json` | A first trial reply from Claude Sonnet 5.5 at low effort, on all 11 original photos, from before the prompt and scorer existed. Not scored. |
